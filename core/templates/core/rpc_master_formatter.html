What gets filled
These fields are pulled from the RPC sheet when available:
NLD date / week
Parsed from the “NLD …” line (exact date/week as shown on the RPC order).
Customer PO#
Parsed from “Customer PO# …”.
RPC#
Parsed from the title line.
City
Parsed from the title line or the address block.
Customer
Parsed from the customer address block.
Bucket qty
Sum of the “Totaal” column for each bucket type.
Bucket Type
Mapped bucket taxonomy (e.g., “10ltr Wide Classic”, “Maxima Buckets”).
Due By / week
Parsed from the “Delivery …” line.
